Revision F4 prototype — series production on hold

Single board: 24.54 × 29.90 mm, 0.8 mm FR-4, two layers, ENIG.
Panel: 3 × 2 boards, 95.62 × 80 mm. Use the Gerber ZIP, BOM and CPL from panel/ for panel orders. Each board has 35 placements, for a total of 210 per panel.

The MIFA is based on TI AN043. The antenna ground and feed points were moved by 3 mm. The extended feed has a ground reference on the bottom layer. Leave L2 unpopulated.

The Y1 crystal body is 5.18 mm from the radiator. XTAL_P and XTAL_N run entirely on the front copper layer without vias. Their total segment lengths, including the connections to the load capacitors, are 6.76 mm and 4.94 mm respectively.

Before series production, test oscillator startup, supply stability during Wi-Fi transmission, reset and USB operation. Verify the regulator orientation in the JLCPCB placement preview. On a board separated from the panel, measure antenna S11 and compare weak-signal reception and packet loss on low, middle and high Wi-Fi channels. RF performance and interference immunity still require prototype measurements.

C13: 1 µF, Samsung CL03A105KQ3CSNC (C318541). Nominal RC time constant: 10 ms.
Header geometry: two rows of eight pins, 20.32 mm between row centers, 2.54 mm pitch. USB interface J1 uses the same geometry as revision F1.
